## HUST personalized-discharge dataset

- **Selected record:** Yuan, Ma, Xu, *The Dataset for: Real-time personalized health status prediction of lithium-ion batteries using deep transfer learning*, Mendeley Data v2, DOI [10.17632/nsc7hnsg4s.2](https://doi.org/10.17632/nsc7hnsg4s.2). Exact artifact `our_data.zip`, file ID `5ca0ac3e-d598-4d07-8dcb-879aa047e98b`, 1,188,136,932 bytes, SHA-256 `071d24617153693b0d29059568525e620f6af6512acc9d00c98c7adcf15125db`. The source is [Mendeley v2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/nsc7hnsg4s/2) and its [public API record](https://data.mendeley.com/public-api/datasets/nsc7hnsg4s).
- **Population and protocol:** the record and original [Ma et al. paper](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2022/ee/d2ee01676a) describe 77 A123 APR18650M1A LFP/graphite cells, 1.1 Ah / 3.3 V nominal, 30 °C, a common fast-charge protocol, and 77 personalized multistage discharge protocols. The paper reports 146,122 discharge cycles and uses discharge capacity as an output. The article does not define a post-formation reference-capacity window for this project.
- **Archive members:** ZIP inspection found exactly 77 `.pkl` files under `our_data/`; the complete member list is in `research/ARCH-01/hust_archive_inventory.json`. All 77 payloads were safely deserialized with the strict pickle-global allowlist for ordered `data`, `dq`, and `rul` label/key checks. In every payload, its nested cell key matches its filename, `data`/`dq`/`rul` keys and order agree, and the first key is 1. Seven payloads—`1-1`, `2-2`, `5-7`, `6-8`, `10-8`, smallest member `2-5`, and largest member `1-2`—were further inspected row-by-row. Across 13,517 frames the same six columns occur: `Status`, `Cycle number`, `Current (mA)`, `Voltage (V)`, `Capacity (mAh)`, `Time (s)`; all fields have zero nulls in these seven. Six status values were observed: CC charge, CC-CV charge, and four numbered CC discharge steps. Frame-level schema/null findings are based on the seven stated cell payloads, not extrapolated to all 77.
- **Capacity semantics from direct comparison:** for every cycle in the seven row-inspected payloads (13,517 total cycle records), `dq` matches `max(Capacity (mAh)) - final Capacity (mAh)` within `2.28e-13 mAh`. This establishes the discharge-capacity difference in mAh for those inspected payloads; the author's [preprocessing code](https://github.com/HAIRLAB/Health_status_prediction/blob/8e9073edbc54c2620b5356e601a5d48abf70573d/common.py) independently uses `dq` as capacity and `rul` as RUL. Positive current occurs in charge statuses and negative current in discharge statuses. `Capacity (mAh)` is a within-test evolving signal, not itself the per-cycle discharge capacity. The author code drops the first nine ordered `dq` labels but does not identify them as formation.
- **Early-cycle sensitivity:** across all 77 ordered label series, the first-three-cycle window range is 0.054%–0.724% of its median (median 0.154%). First-cycle deviation from the first-three median is −0.314% to +0.619% (median +0.101%). For first-five, first-ten, and first-thirty windows, maximum within-window ranges are 0.830%, 1.289%, and 1.961% respectively. These observed comparisons support a reproducible three-cycle operational baseline; they do not relabel HUST cycles as source-declared formation. The paper's tenth-cycle baseline applies to charge-curve features, not the SOH denominator.
- **License and redistribution:** the exact [Mendeley v2 record](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/nsc7hnsg4s/2) displays CC BY 4.0. The [CC BY 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en) grants reproduction and sharing of licensed material, including database contents, with attribution, license link, and change indication. Mendeley notes further permission may be needed for content identified as third-party; the exact archive contains only the 77 pickle members and no per-member license/third-party manifest or identified third-party component. Therefore record-level redistribution is permitted under CC BY 4.0 for material the dataset contributors licensed; do not extend that permission to separately identified third-party content. The RSC article's license is separate.

## Empa Aurora dataset

- **Selected record:** Svaluto-Ferro et al., *Dataset for publication “Toward an Autonomous Robotic Battery Materials Research Platform…”*, Zenodo v1, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.15481956](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15481956). Exact artifact `Dataset-rocrate.zip`, 2,507,129,091 bytes; source-record MD5 and locally computed MD5 are both `eaec9549b74b59d998e5138dab965b5d`; locally computed SHA-256 is `61f66d309462c5bcbb00ddfef0d141ad836f811f2b78d07457aadc4a3e7baff7`. See the [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/15481956) and [record API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/15481956).
- **Archive and exact V1 subset:** the complete archive contains 797 ZIP entries: 199 cell folders with one JSON-LD metadata file, one BDF CSV, and one BDF Parquet each, plus `ro-crate-metadata.json`. Metadata inspection of all 199 cells gives 135 NMC622//graphite (`LiNi0.6Co0.2Mn0.2O2`), 32 graphite cells whose JSON-LD positive formula is `Ni0.83Mn0.06Co0.11O2`, and 32 LFP//graphite (`LiFePO4`) cells. The original paper identifies NMC811 as `LiNi0.83Mn0.10Co0.07O2`; because the archive metadata's Mn/Co proportions differ, those 32 records are not labeled NMC811 here pending clarification. The exact LFP IDs are `ccid000217`–`ccid000247` excluding `ccid000248`, plus `ccid000249` (32 IDs; enumerated in `research/ARCH-01/aurora_archive_inventory.json`). The paper describes a batch of 36 LFP cells, while this Zenodo v1 archive has 32 LFP cell records; this is a source-population difference, not silently reconciled here.
- **BDF schema:** all 199 Parquet files have one schema and 99,715,350 total rows. The six observed fields and Arrow types are `test_time_millisecond` (int64), `current_ampere` (float64), `voltage_volt` (float64), `cycle_dimensionless` (int64), `date_time_millisecond` (int64), and `ambient_temperature_celsius` (float64). No field is a charge/discharge capacity or energy. Parquet statistics report zero nulls in each field for all 199 files. CSV/Parquet schemas and values were identical for `ccid000001`, `ccid000037`, `ccid000210`, and `ccid000249` (first/last sampled IDs across chemistries). The CSV and Parquet are advertised as identical in the record; only those four were value-compared directly.
- **Cycle and protocol evidence:** the source `cycle_dimensionless` starts at zero and repeatedly resets; preserve its raw values and do not treat it as the logical cycle number. Direct JSON-LD metadata for every one of the 32 LFP cells specifies three iterations at 0.1 mA cm⁻² followed by a 1,000-iteration aging loop at 1.0 mA cm⁻², with a 3.65 V charge limit, CV termination at 0.05 mA cm⁻², and discharge to 2.5 V. This protocol evidence is LFP-specific and resolves the earlier concern that the paper's NMC622 protocol could not be generalized. In all 32 Parquet traces, ordered contiguous negative-current events with integrated capacity at least 0.1 mAh yield exactly 1,003 events, matching the metadata loops. Their measured capacity distribution has a >64× gap from zero-duration/singleton artifacts (maximum below cut 0.00428 mAh; minimum above cut 0.2757 mAh). Integration is trapezoidal over absolute current vs elapsed milliseconds. This supplies a reproducible event/capacity derivation; retain the native reset counter and quarantine events failing protocol/cutoff quality checks. Three cells have one substantial event each ending more than 0.08 V from the 2.5 V cutoff diagnostic; their first three sustained-aging reference events align with cutoff.
- **License:** Zenodo v1 API declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en). Preserve attribution, license link, and change indication. This is record-level licensing; retain the record's creator attribution and provenance. Do not represent nominal/rated capacity metadata as measured capacity.
