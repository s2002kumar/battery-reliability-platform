# Data Sources — ARCH-01 Verification State

**Gate status: BLOCKED.** The exact HUST and Aurora archives were downloaded outside the repository and checked against their source-record identities. The HUST capacity label is now supported by direct value comparisons in five complete cell payloads. Aurora metadata and all 199 Parquet schemas were inspected; there is no explicit capacity field, and the LFP cycle index repeatedly resets to zero. A source-compatible, deterministic SOH denominator and Aurora cycle-to-capacity mapping are not approved. No ingestion is authorized.

No battery data bytes are stored in Git. Archive URLs, versions, sizes, checksums, license evidence, and audit outputs are recorded in [ARCH-01 evidence](docs/evidence/ARCH-01.md). SHA-256 values below are locally computed. Mendeley exposes no upstream SHA-256 for HUST; Aurora's record publishes MD5, which was matched locally.

## HUST personalized-discharge dataset

- **Selected record:** Yuan, Ma, Xu, *The Dataset for: Real-time personalized health status prediction of lithium-ion batteries using deep transfer learning*, Mendeley Data v2, DOI [10.17632/nsc7hnsg4s.2](https://doi.org/10.17632/nsc7hnsg4s.2). Exact artifact `our_data.zip`, file ID `5ca0ac3e-d598-4d07-8dcb-879aa047e98b`, 1,188,136,932 bytes, SHA-256 `071d24617153693b0d29059568525e620f6af6512acc9d00c98c7adcf15125db`. The source is [Mendeley v2](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/nsc7hnsg4s/2) and its [public API record](https://data.mendeley.com/public-api/datasets/nsc7hnsg4s).
- **Population and protocol:** the record and original [Ma et al. paper](https://pubs.rsc.org/en/content/articlehtml/2022/ee/d2ee01676a) describe 77 A123 APR18650M1A LFP/graphite cells, 1.1 Ah / 3.3 V nominal, 30 °C, a common fast-charge protocol, and 77 personalized multistage discharge protocols. The paper reports 146,122 discharge cycles and uses discharge capacity as an output. The article does not define a post-formation reference-capacity window for this project.
- **Archive members:** ZIP inspection found exactly 77 `.pkl` files under `our_data/`; the complete member list is in `research/ARCH-01/hust_archive_inventory.json`. Five edge/representative payloads were safely deserialized: `1-1`, `2-2`, `5-7`, `6-8`, and `10-8`. Each has a one-cell top-level key equal to its cell identifier and nested `data`, `dq`, and `rul` objects. Their `data` cycle mappings and `dq` labels have matching counts: 1,400–2,651 cycles per selected cell, and each cycle DataFrame has the same six columns: `Status`, `Cycle number`, `Current (mA)`, `Voltage (V)`, `Capacity (mAh)`, `Time (s)`. Six status values were observed: CC charge, CC-CV charge, and four numbered CC discharge steps. The stored `Cycle number` equals the containing cycle key in these five payloads.
- **Capacity semantics from direct comparison:** for every cycle in each of the five inspected payloads (9,453 total cycle records), `dq` matches `max(Capacity (mAh)) - final Capacity (mAh)` within `2.28e-13 mAh`. This establishes the relation and mAh scale in these five files, not yet every one of the 77. Positive current occurs in charge statuses and negative current in discharge statuses in these samples. `Capacity (mAh)` rises during charge and declines during discharge; it is not itself the cycle discharge capacity. The project can preserve `dq` as a source-specific capacity label, but source-wide adapter approval waits on the uninspected 72 payloads.
- **Early-cycle evidence:** across those five cells, first-cycle `dq` is 0.233–0.283% above the median of cycles 1–10; all five decline across the observed first ten. Author preprocessing code at [HAIRLAB/Health_status_prediction](https://github.com/HAIRLAB/Health_status_prediction/blob/main/common.py), inspected at commit `8e9073edbc54c2620b5356e601a5d48abf70573d`, slices off the first nine ordered `dq` keys. It does not document this as a formation rule. The HUST paper also uses the tenth cycle as a charge-feature curve baseline, which does not establish a capacity reference rule.
- **License:** the authoritative Mendeley record declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en). Attribution, license link, and change indication are required for sharing/adaptation. Mendeley's record/API also carries a general warning that additional permission may be needed for content identified as third-party. The archive contains no README or license member identifying such content; absence of an embedded notice does not clear third-party rights. Do not redistribute source members until any applicable third-party content/rights are clarified. The RSC article's own license is not the dataset license.

## Empa Aurora dataset

- **Selected record:** Svaluto-Ferro et al., *Dataset for publication “Toward an Autonomous Robotic Battery Materials Research Platform…”*, Zenodo v1, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.15481956](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15481956). Exact artifact `Dataset-rocrate.zip`, 2,507,129,091 bytes; source-record MD5 and locally computed MD5 are both `eaec9549b74b59d998e5138dab965b5d`; locally computed SHA-256 is `61f66d309462c5bcbb00ddfef0d141ad836f811f2b78d07457aadc4a3e7baff7`. See the [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/15481956) and [record API](https://zenodo.org/api/records/15481956).
- **Archive and exact V1 subset:** the complete archive contains 797 ZIP entries: 199 cell folders with one JSON-LD metadata file, one BDF CSV, and one BDF Parquet each, plus `ro-crate-metadata.json`. Metadata inspection of all 199 cells gives 135 NMC622//graphite (`LiNi0.6Co0.2Mn0.2O2`), 32 graphite cells whose JSON-LD positive formula is `Ni0.83Mn0.06Co0.11O2`, and 32 LFP//graphite (`LiFePO4`) cells. The original paper identifies NMC811 as `LiNi0.83Mn0.10Co0.07O2`; because the archive metadata's Mn/Co proportions differ, those 32 records are not labeled NMC811 here pending clarification. The exact LFP IDs are `ccid000217`–`ccid000247` excluding `ccid000248`, plus `ccid000249` (32 IDs; enumerated in `research/ARCH-01/aurora_archive_inventory.json`). The paper describes a batch of 36 LFP cells, while this Zenodo v1 archive has 32 LFP cell records; this is a source-population difference, not silently reconciled here.
- **BDF schema:** all 199 Parquet files have one schema and 99,715,350 total rows. The six observed fields and Arrow types are `test_time_millisecond` (int64), `current_ampere` (float64), `voltage_volt` (float64), `cycle_dimensionless` (int64), `date_time_millisecond` (int64), and `ambient_temperature_celsius` (float64). No field is a charge/discharge capacity or energy. Parquet statistics report zero nulls in each field for all 199 files. CSV/Parquet schemas and values were identical for `ccid000001`, `ccid000037`, `ccid000210`, and `ccid000249` (first/last sampled IDs across chemistries). The CSV and Parquet are advertised as identical in the record; only those four were value-compared directly.
- **Cycle and protocol evidence:** all inspected files begin at `cycle_dimensionless=0`; the maxima vary by cell. The 32 LFP records have maxima of 1003. In representative LFP `ccid000217`, the sequence transitions to zero 506 times after increasing above zero, including interleaved returns such as `0,1,2,3,0,3,4,0,4`; the record has no step index, status, or capacity field to resolve those boundaries. The original [Aurora paper](https://chemistry-europe.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/batt.202500155) reports three formation cycles at 0.1 mA cm⁻² followed by long-term cycling at 1.0 mA cm⁻² for its NMC622 case; do not generalize that phase count to LFP. The LFP discussion gives a 3.65 V upper cutoff. In directly inspected LFP `ccid000217` and `ccid000249`, cycles 1–3 have current magnitudes around 0.154 mA and cycle 4 onward around 1.54 mA, consistent with a current-level change; the cycle-index resets still prevent an approved complete-cycle/capacity mapping. Per-cell JSON-LD records encode cell-specific protocols, rated-capacity metadata, electrode composition, and identifiers.
- **License:** Zenodo v1 API declares [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode.en). Preserve attribution, license link, and change indication. This is record-level licensing; retain the record's creator attribution and provenance. Do not represent nominal/rated capacity metadata as measured capacity.

## Provenance and disposition

Future artifact manifests must retain source record/version, exact source file key, UTC retrieval timestamp, locally computed SHA-256, byte size, adapter/version, exact license identifier, attribution, and source-specific interpretation. Record MD5 is retained as upstream identity evidence, not substituted for SHA-256.

HUST and Aurora remain the selected research sources; neither is approved for ingestion yet. HUST's sampled `dq` semantics are supported, but all 77 files and third-party rights are not fully cleared. Aurora's actual time-series schema contains no capacity field and its LFP cycle count resets are unresolved. BDA/SINTEF/DLR exact files are licensed candidates at record/catalog level, but their bytes remain unparsed. ARCH-00 and ARCH-01 stay BLOCKED; no downstream work is unblocked.
